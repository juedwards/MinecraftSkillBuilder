# Build a Home for the Village

Age range: 8–13 · Subject links: History, Design & Technology, Art

## Learning aims

Students understand how buildings in a settlement share a style shaped by the people,
place and materials of the time. They design a home that fits in with an existing village
and meets the needs of the family who will live there.

## Learning objectives

By the end of the task the student can:

1. **Match the style.** Use the same kinds of materials, roof shapes and details as the
   buildings around them.
2. **Build a sound structure.** Make walls, a roof, a door and windows that form a complete,
   enclosed home.
3. **Design for the people living there.** Include a furnished interior with places to
   sleep, cook or work, and store things.
4. **Fit the plot.** Use the plot well, connect the entrance to the street, and keep the
   building within the plot.
5. **Add character.** Include details (lighting, gardens, decoration) that make the home
   look lived in.

## Task

The villagers need a new home on the empty plot in front of you. Look at the village
around you, then build a home that fits in with its style, with a roof, a door facing the
street, windows and a furnished inside. Type **finished** in chat when you are done.

## Starter village

A small medieval farming village with timber-framed cottages of oak and cobblestone,
thatched-looking roofs, a blacksmith, a bakery, a church with a bell tower, small fenced
farm plots and a stone well in the square.

## Assessment criteria

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Matching the village style | Materials and shapes do not match the village. | Some materials or features match. | Materials, roof and details clearly match the village. | Fits in naturally, and adds a creative twist that still suits the style. |
| Structure | Incomplete walls or no roof. | Mostly complete, with gaps or no door or windows. | Complete walls, roof, door and windows. | Well-proportioned, sturdy-looking structure with a carefully shaped roof. |
| Interior and function | Empty inside. | One or two furnishings. | A furnished interior with places to sleep and cook or work. | A thoughtfully planned interior with distinct areas and storage. |
| Use of the plot | Spills outside the plot or ignores the street. | Inside the plot, but the entrance doesn't face the street. | Inside the plot with the entrance connected to the street. | Uses the whole plot well, e.g. a garden or path that links to the street. |
| Detail and character | No detail. | A little decoration. | Several details such as lighting, flowers or fences. | Rich details that tell a story about who lives there. |

## Notes for the assessor

Compare the student's home with the village buildings described in the starting scene.
Give formative feedback: praise specific strengths, explain each level, and give one or
two concrete next steps.
